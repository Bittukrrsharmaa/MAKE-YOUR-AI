1. PURPOSE
These guidelines summarize common employee administration and workplace expectations.

2. EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING
New employees should complete the joining forms, identity and payroll documentation, policy acknowledgements, and mandatory training assigned by HR.
Employees receive access credentials and equipment according to their role. Credentials must never be shared.

3. WORKING HOURS AND ATTENDANCE
The sample standard schedule is Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, with a reasonable meal break. Actual schedules may vary by team, shift, location, or contract.
Employees must record attendance using the approved attendance system and inform their manager if they expect to be late or absent.

4. HYBRID AND REMOTE WORK
Remote or hybrid work requires manager approval and must follow information-security requirements.
Employees should protect company devices, use approved networks and tools, and remain reachable during agreed working hours.

5. CODE OF CONDUCT
Employees are expected to:
- Treat colleagues, customers, and partners respectfully.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, bullying, and retaliation.
- Disclose conflicts of interest.
- Protect confidential company and customer information.
- Use company resources responsibly.
- Follow applicable laws and company policies.

6. PERFORMANCE AND FEEDBACK
Managers should communicate role expectations, provide regular feedback, and document performance discussions. Employees may request clarification or development support.

7. LEARNING AND DEVELOPMENT
Training may include role-specific learning, security awareness, compliance, communication, and leadership development. Course approval and reimbursement depend on the applicable learning policy.

8. RESIGNATION AND EXIT
Employees who resign should submit written notice according to their employment agreement. HR coordinates knowledge transfer, asset return, access closure, final settlement, and exit feedback.

9. CONFIDENTIALITY
Employee records and personal information must be accessed only by authorized personnel for legitimate business purposes.

10. HR SUPPORT
For payroll, attendance, workplace concerns, or employee-record corrections, raise a request through the official HR helpdesk. Include the employee ID only in approved secure systems.
