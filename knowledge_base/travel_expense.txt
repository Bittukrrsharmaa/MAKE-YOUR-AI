COMPANY KNOWLEDGE BASE
DOCUMENT: BUSINESS TRAVEL AND EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
VERSION: 1.0
NOTE: Fictional sample policy for chatbot/RAG testing. All amounts and limits below are illustrative, not actual company entitlements.

1. PURPOSE
This document explains how employees request business travel approval and claim eligible expenses.

2. PRE-APPROVAL
- Obtain manager approval before booking business travel.
- State the business purpose, destination, dates, estimated cost, and project or cost centre.
- Use the approved travel desk or booking process.
- Personal extensions or upgrades require separate approval and may be at the employee's expense.

3. ILLUSTRATIVE DAILY LIMITS
These sample limits are for testing only and must be replaced with the real company's approved rates:
- Meals: up to INR 1,000 per day.
- Local transport: actual reasonable cost with receipt.
- Hotel: up to INR 4,000 per night, subject to destination and availability.
- Air or rail: approved class and fare based on role and trip requirements.
Actual reimbursement is subject to approval, receipts, location rules, and the company's current policy.

4. ELIGIBLE EXPENSES
Reasonable and necessary expenses directly related to approved business travel may be reimbursed. Examples include approved transport, accommodation, meals, and business-related incidental costs.

5. NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES
Personal shopping, leisure activities, traffic fines, unauthorized upgrades, minibar charges, and expenses without a valid business purpose are generally not reimbursable unless an exception is formally approved.

6. CLAIM SUBMISSION
1. Open the approved expense-management portal.
2. Select the relevant trip or business purpose.
3. Enter each expense date, category, amount, currency, and description.
4. Upload clear itemized receipts.
5. Attach required approval or travel documents.
6. Submit within the company's stated deadline.
7. Track the claim and respond to finance queries.

7. RECEIPTS
Receipts should show the merchant, date, items or service, and amount. If a receipt is unavailable, provide an explanation and follow the finance team's exception process.

8. CURRENCY CONVERSION
For foreign-currency expenses, use the conversion method and exchange-rate source specified by Finance. Attach the original receipt and record the amount and currency paid.

9. CANCELLATIONS AND CHANGES
Notify the travel desk and manager promptly. Explain the business reason for any cancellation or change. Refunds and cancellation charges are handled according to booking terms and company policy.

10. APPROVAL AND PAYMENT
Claims are reviewed by the manager and Finance. Approved claims are reimbursed through the normal payroll or accounts-payable cycle. Incomplete or non-compliant claims may be returned for correction.

11. QUESTIONS
Contact the Finance or travel helpdesk through the official internal channel for claim status, policy exceptions, or clarification.
