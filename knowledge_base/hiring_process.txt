1. PURPOSE
This document explains the standard recruitment process for full-time employees.

2. ELIGIBILITY
- Applicants must meet the education and experience requirements listed in the job description.
- Fresh graduates may apply for entry-level roles.
- Applicants must provide accurate education, employment, and contact details.
- Some roles may require a portfolio, coding assessment, certification, or work sample.

3. HIRING STAGES
Stage 1 - Application:
Candidates submit a resume through the company careers portal or an authorized recruiter.

Stage 2 - Resume Screening:
The recruitment team checks skills, education, experience, and role requirements. Shortlisted applicants are contacted by email or phone.

Stage 3 - Initial HR Discussion:
The recruiter discusses the role, location, work arrangement, notice period, salary expectations, and availability.

Stage 4 - Assessment:
Depending on the role, candidates may complete an aptitude test, coding test, case study, writing task, or practical assignment.

Stage 5 - Technical or Functional Interviews:
The hiring panel evaluates job-related knowledge, problem-solving, communication, and practical skills. There may be one or more rounds.

Stage 6 - Managerial Interview:
The hiring manager assesses role fit, collaboration, ownership, and expectations.

Stage 7 - HR and Compensation Discussion:
HR explains the proposed compensation, benefits, joining date, and employment conditions.

Stage 8 - Background Verification:
The company may verify identity, education, employment history, and references with the candidate's consent, as permitted by applicable law.

Stage 9 - Offer:
Selected candidates receive a written offer. The candidate should review and accept it by the stated deadline.

Stage 10 - Onboarding:
After acceptance and completion of required checks, HR shares joining instructions and onboarding documents.

4. TYPICAL DOCUMENTS
- Updated resume
- Government-approved identity and address proof, where legally required
- Education certificates and mark sheets
- Previous employment and experience letters, if applicable
- Recent photographs, if requested
- Bank and tax details during onboarding, through approved secure channels

5. CANDIDATE COMMUNICATION
Recruitment updates are shared through official company email or the authorized recruitment portal. Candidates should not pay recruitment fees. The company will not request passwords or OTPs.

6. RESCHEDULING
Candidates who cannot attend an interview should notify the recruiter as early as possible and suggest alternative time slots.

7. QUESTIONS
For application status or interview scheduling, contact the recruiter using the official contact details in the job posting.
