COMPANY KNOWLEDGE BASE
DOCUMENT: LEAVE POLICY
VERSION: 1.0
NOTE: Fictional example only. The leave balances below are illustrative and are not legal or actual company entitlements.

1. PURPOSE
This document describes example leave categories and the process for requesting time off.

2. ILLUSTRATIVE ANNUAL LEAVE ALLOCATION
- Casual Leave (CL): 12 days per calendar year.
- Sick Leave (SL): 10 days per calendar year.
- Earned/Privilege Leave (EL/PL): 15 days per calendar year, accrued monthly.
These figures are sample values. Actual eligibility, accrual, carry-forward, and encashment are governed by the employee's contract and applicable law.

3. CASUAL LEAVE
Casual leave may be used for personal work or short-notice needs. Employees should request it in advance where possible. For emergencies, notify the manager as soon as practical.

4. SICK LEAVE
Sick leave may be used when an employee is unwell or needs medical recovery. The company may request supporting documentation for extended absence, consistent with law and policy.

5. EARNED OR PRIVILEGE LEAVE
Earned leave is accrued over time and is generally intended for planned vacations or personal commitments. Submit requests early so the team can plan coverage.

6. REQUEST PROCESS
1. Open the approved HR or attendance portal.
2. Select the leave category and dates.
3. Add a brief reason if required.
4. Submit the request to the reporting manager.
5. Check the portal for approval or rejection.
6. Ensure handover of urgent work before leave begins.

7. CANCELLATION OR CHANGE
If plans change, withdraw or modify the request in the HR portal and inform the manager. Approval of changes depends on staffing and business needs.

8. HOLIDAYS AND WEEKENDS
Holiday and weekly-off treatment depends on the employee's location, shift, and applicable holiday calendar. Check the official calendar before submitting a request.

9. UNPAID LEAVE
If paid leave is unavailable or exhausted, an employee may request unpaid leave. Approval is discretionary and subject to applicable law and company rules.

10. QUESTIONS
Contact HR through the official helpdesk for leave-balance corrections, policy interpretation, or portal issues.
