COMPANY KNOWLEDGE BASE
DOCUMENT: MEDICAL AND HEALTH BENEFITS POLICY
VERSION: 1.0
NOTE: Fictional sample policy for chatbot/RAG testing. It does not promise actual insurance coverage or replace an insurer's policy wording.

1. PURPOSE
This document gives a general overview of example employee medical benefits.

2. ELIGIBILITY
Eligibility may depend on employment type, joining date, location, and the insurer's terms. HR provides the current plan summary and dependent-enrolment rules.

3. SAMPLE COVERAGE
A company-sponsored group health plan may cover eligible employees and enrolled dependents for approved hospitalization and other benefits stated in the insurance schedule.
Coverage limits, room category, waiting periods, exclusions, co-payments, and network hospitals are determined by the insurer's policy.

4. ENROLMENT
Eligible employees should submit enrolment details and dependent documents within the stated enrolment window. Report changes in dependent information to HR promptly.

5. CASHLESS HOSPITALIZATION
1. Check whether the hospital is in the insurer's network.
2. Present the health card or policy details at the insurance desk.
3. The hospital submits a pre-authorization request.
4. The insurer reviews the request and communicates its decision.
5. The employee may need to pay non-covered expenses.

Cashless treatment is subject to insurer approval and policy conditions.

6. REIMBURSEMENT CLAIM
1. Notify the insurer or TPA within the required timeline.
2. Complete the claim form.
3. Submit original bills, prescriptions, discharge summary, investigation reports, and other requested documents.
4. Keep copies of all submitted documents.
5. Track the claim using the insurer's reference number.

7. EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS
Not every treatment or expense is covered. Common limitations may include non-medical items, excluded procedures, waiting periods, sub-limits, and expenses above approved limits. Refer to the current insurer policy for the definitive list.

8. EMERGENCY
In a medical emergency, seek immediate medical care first. Contact the insurer or emergency support line as soon as practical.

9. PRIVACY
Medical information is sensitive. Share it only through authorized insurer or HR channels and only when necessary.

10. SUPPORT
For policy numbers, e-cards, network hospitals, claim status, or coverage clarification, contact the insurer/TPA using the current official contact details or raise an HR helpdesk request.
